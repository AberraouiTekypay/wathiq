## 2. ITAD (IT Asset Disposition) Operating Framework

When a corporation decommissions a fleet of devices, Wathiq provides a closed-loop chain of custody:

```text
    1. AGREEMENT          2. COLLECTION           3. DOCK INTAKE
  ┌──────────────┐       ┌──────────────┐        ┌──────────────┐
  │ ITAD Master  │──────►│ Secure Van   │───────►│ Casablanca   │
  │ Contract     │       │ Manifest     │        │ Central Hub  │
  └──────────────┘       └──────────────┘        └──────────────┘
                                                        │
                         5. REFURBISH                   │ 4. PURGE
  ┌──────────────┐       ┌──────────────┐        ┌──────▼───────┐
  │ Residual     │◄──────│ Diagnostic,  │◄───────│ NIST 800-88  │
  │ Valuation    │       │ Repair & QA  │        │ Mass Wipe    │
  └──────────────┘       └──────────────┘        └──────────────┘
         │
         ▼
  6. RESALE / SECOND LIFECYCLE
```

### 2.1 Chain of Custody & Collection Manifest
Every physical pickup produces a formal **Collection Manifest** (e.g. `MNF-BOA-2026-089`):
* Enumerates physical chassis count, device serials, and collection depot.
* Requires dual physical signatures: Client IT Director and Wathiq Logistics Driver.
* Unit custody transfers into Wathiq OS in the `EXPECTED` state.

### 2.2 Residual Valuation & Financial Settlement
After intake, diagnostics, and data sanitization, Wathiq generates the **ITAD Recovery Settlement Report**:
$$\text{Net Credit Payable to Client} = \sum (\text{Fair Market Value}) - (\text{Collection Freight} + \text{Data Wipe Fees})$$
For Bank of Africa Batch `ITAD-CAS-2026-014` (284 devices):
* Gross asset recovery value: **1,020,000 MAD**
* Processing, logistics, and data sanitization fees: **130,000 MAD**
* **Net Value Returned to Client:** **890,000 MAD** (credited toward new certified fleet hardware).

### 2.3 Environmental Impact & ESG Reporting
Morocco has committed to aggressive green transformation and carbon neutrality targets. Wathiq calculates the avoided environmental cost for every enterprise ITAD contract:
* Extending the working life of one laptop prevents approximately **150 kg of $\text{CO}_2$ emissions** and saves **1,500 liters of water** compared to manufacturing a new laptop.
* Bank of Africa Batch (284 laptops): **42.6 Metric Tons of $\text{CO}_2$ avoided**.

## 3. B2B Quotations & Moroccan Tax (TVA) Structure

All corporate quotes generated by Wathiq OS adhere strictly to the Moroccan General Tax Code (Code Général des Impôts):
* **Moroccan Standard TVA Rate:** **20%**
* Mandatory billing information:
  * Client Company Name & Legal Address
  * Client **ICE** (Identifiant Commun de l’Entreprise - 15 digits)
  * Client **IF** (Identifiant Fiscal)
  * Client **RC** (Registre du Commerce)
  * Client **Patente**
